# README.md

## Project Goal

Create a clean, modern landing page for a consulting business focused on:
- Operational systems for SMBs
- Custom internal tools
- AI-assisted workflows
- Apple ecosystem consulting
- Cybersecurity-aware business automation

The positioning should feel:
- Practical
- Operational
- Enterprise-grade
- Trustworthy
- Not hype-driven
- Not like a generic AI agency

Tone:
- Experienced operator
- Direct
- Technical but business-friendly
- Focused on outcomes

Avoid:
- Generic AI buzzwords
- Robot imagery
- “Revolutionizing business” language
- Overly futuristic marketing
- Corporate jargon

Visual direction:
- Apple-inspired simplicity
- White/light neutral background
- Minimal color palette
- Clean typography
- Lots of spacing
- Strong hierarchy
- Professional but modern

Potential accent colors:
- Deep graphite
- Soft blue
- Muted orange (minimal)

Suggested sections:
1. Hero
2. Problems solved
3. What I build
4. Process
5. Example use cases
6. About
7. CTA/contact

## Intro Text
Most small businesses already know where their bottlenecks are.

The challenge has historically been that solving them required enterprise-level budgets, developers, or large internal teams.

That barrier no longer exists.

---

## Problem Cards

### Repetitive Admin Work
Teams wasting hours copying information between systems, spreadsheets, emails, or CRMs.

### Disconnected Information
Critical business data spread across multiple tools with no centralized workflow.

### Manual Processes
Operations that still depend on repetitive human input for predictable tasks.

### Workflow Bottlenecks
Key employees becoming operational choke points because systems are not scalable.

### Tool Overload
Businesses paying for software that employees barely use or that does not integrate properly.

### Security Risks
AI and automation tools being implemented without proper access control or operational safeguards.
